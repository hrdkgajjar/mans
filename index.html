Who We Are

Mahila Atyachar Nivaran Samiti is a beacon of hope and strength, passionately dedicated to safeguarding women from the shadows of domestic violence, harassment, abuse, and discrimination. We stand firm in our commitment to championing the dignity, equality, and empowerment of every woman. Through comprehensive legal awareness, vital education, and unwavering support, we strive to dismantle injustice and build a society where women's rights are not just recognized, but revered.

What We Do

We provide a circle of support through a range of dedicated services.

Domestic Violence Support

Offering a safe haven with immediate assistance, counseling, and shelter information for victims of domestic abuse.

Women's Legal Guidance

Providing expert legal advice and assistance to help women understand and exercise their rights effectively.

Family Counseling

Facilitating constructive dialogue and resolution for family disputes in a confidential and supportive environment.

Community Awareness

Conducting workshops and seminars to educate communities on gender equality, legal rights, and digital safety.
