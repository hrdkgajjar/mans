Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Mahila Atyachar Nivaran Samiti ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and phone number when you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact, volunteer, or membership forms.

3. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide support.
  • Process your volunteer or membership applications.
  • Send you updates about our events and campaigns, with your consent.
  • Improve our website and services.

4. Confidentiality and Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. All information related to cases of harassment, violence, or abuse is treated with the strictest confidentiality and is not shared without explicit consent, except as required by law.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

6. Your Consent

By using our site, you consent to our website's privacy policy.

7. Contacting Us

If there are any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us using the information on our contact page.
