About Our Organization

A Journey of Compassion, Justice, and Empowerment

Our Story

Founded on the principles of justice and equality, Mahila Atyachar Nivaran Samiti began as a grassroots movement to address the silent suffering of women in our communities. President Kokilaben Gajjar, witnessing the pervasive challenges of domestic violence, harassment, and discrimination, resolved to create a safe and empowering space for women. From a small group of dedicated volunteers, we have grown into a trusted organization, providing critical support and legal guidance to thousands, and tirelessly advocating for systemic change.

Our journey is one of resilience and hope, fueled by the courage of the women we serve and the unwavering support of our community.

Our Mission

To create a society where every woman lives with dignity, free from fear and violence. We are committed to providing comprehensive support to women in distress, advocating for their rights, and fostering a culture of gender equality through legal aid, counseling, and community awareness.

Our Vision

A future where women are empowered, their voices are heard, and their rights are protected. We envision a just and equitable society where women are active participants in all spheres of life, contributing to a stronger and more compassionate world.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every action we take.

Respect

Upholding the dignity of every individual.

Equality

Championing equal rights and opportunities for all.

Justice

Ensuring fair and equitable access to legal systems.

Compassion

Providing empathetic and non-judgmental support.

Integrity

Operating with transparency and ethical standards.

Empowerment

Enabling women to build self-reliant futures.

Safety

Creating a secure environment for women to seek help.

Confidentiality

Protecting the privacy of those we serve.
