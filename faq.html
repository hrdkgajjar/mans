Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our work and how you can get involved.

How can I get help from your organization?

If you or someone you know needs help, please call us at +91 98257 28291 or visit our contact page to send a confidential message. If you are in immediate danger, please call 181 or 100.

Are your services free of charge?

Yes, our counseling, guidance, and support services are provided completely free of charge. For legal representation, we connect women with free legal aid services or trusted, low-cost legal professionals.

Is my information kept confidential?

Absolutely. Confidentiality is one of our core values. All conversations, personal details, and cases are handled with the utmost privacy and discretion. We will never share your information without your explicit consent.

How can I volunteer?

We welcome dedicated individuals to join our cause. Please visit our Volunteer page to see the available roles and fill out the application form. We would love to have you on our team!

How are donations used?

Your donations are vital to our work. They are used to fund our awareness programs, provide counseling services, cover operational costs for legal aid camps, and support our outreach to rural communities. Every contribution helps us empower more women.
